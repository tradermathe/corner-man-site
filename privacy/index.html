Cornerman

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 May 2026 · Version 1

Cornerman ("Cornerman", the "App", "we", "us", "our") is a mobile application that records your boxing sessions on your phone, analyses your movement, and gives you feedback. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Cornerman is provided by Mathe Wieme, an individual based in the United Arab Emirates. You can reach us at support@corner-man.ai.

The short version. The App records video and audio of you training and analyses your movement on your device. We upload that recording and the movement data from it to our cloud and use it to improve and train the models that power the App. Your account is identified by the email address you use to sign in, so you can reach your data across devices — we don't ask for your name or phone number, and we do not sell your data or run ads. You can ask us to delete everything at any time.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Mathe Wieme (United Arab Emirates) is the controller responsible for the personal data processed through Cornerman. Questions or requests: support@corner-man.ai.

2. What we collect

Session recordings

When you record a session, the App captures video and audio of you training using your device's camera and microphone. The audio includes the sound of your strikes, which helps the App detect and time your punches.

Movement and analysis data

From each recording the App derives body-position ("pose" or "skeleton") data, on-device analysis results (such as punch counts and form metrics), and, in guided sessions, a timeline of the drills and cues that were called out.

Account and identifiers

A login is required to use the App. You can sign in with Google, Apple, or an email address and password. However you sign in, the App collects your email address and assigns a user identifier so your data can be stored and synced across sessions and devices. Your email address is linked to your account and the data associated with it. We do not collect your name or phone number.

If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your email address from that provider but never your password. If you sign in with an email and password, the password is handled by our authentication provider and stored only in hashed form — we never see it in plain text.

Preferences and consent

The App stores the preferences you choose during setup (such as your stance, skill level, and training focus) and a record that you accepted this Privacy Policy, including the date and version.

Technical data

Our service providers process standard technical information needed to operate the App, such as app version, device type, network requests, and timestamps.

3. How we use your data

  • To run the App — recording sessions and analysing your movement. Most analysis happens directly on your device.
  • To store and sync your sessions so they are available to you.
  • To improve and train our machine-learning models. We use your uploaded recordings and the movement data derived from them to develop, evaluate, and retrain the models that power Cornerman's analysis and feedback.
  • To maintain, secure, and debug the service.

We do not use your data for third-party advertising, and we do not sell your personal data.

4. On-device processing

Cornerman's movement analysis runs on your phone. Uploading your recordings and derived data to our cloud is what enables storage, sync, and the model-improvement use described above.

5. Who we share data with

We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Realtime Database, and Cloud Storage) to host and process data on our behalf. Their handling of data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. We share data with service providers only as needed to run the App, and we may disclose data where required by law.

6. International transfers

We are based in the United Arab Emirates, and our service providers may store and process your data on servers located in other countries. Where data is transferred internationally, we rely on our providers' safeguards for protecting it.

7. How long we keep your data

We keep your recordings and derived data for as long as needed to provide the App and to develop and improve our models, unless you ask us to delete them.

8. Your rights and choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your data, or to stop using it to improve our models, by emailing support@corner-man.ai. To help us locate your data, please contact us from the email address associated with your account.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021), the EU/UK GDPR, or the California Consumer Privacy Act. We honour these rights where they apply to you.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, including the security features of our cloud providers, to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

Cornerman is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has used the App, contact us and we will delete the data.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If we make a material change to how we handle your data, we will ask you to review and accept the updated policy in the App before you continue recording.
